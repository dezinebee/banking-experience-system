## How this phase was produced

- Live web research (August 2026) into: Visa Product Design System, GOV.UK Design System, Salesforce Lightning (SLDS 2), Adobe Spectrum, IBM Carbon, USWDS, Atlassian, Finastra (RTL); UAE banking landscape (ENBD X, FAB/Payit, ADCB Hayyak, Mashreq Neo, DIB, Emirates Islamic, Wio, Liv X, Zand, Mbank, ruya, Careem Pay, Botim, e& money, exchange houses, BNPL); UAE regulation (CBUAE Consumer Protection Regulation & Standards, Open Finance / Al Tareq / Nebras, UAE PASS, KYC/AML, Fraud Notice 2025/3057, PDPL, National Policy for Digital Accessibility, Higher Shari'ah Authority disclosure standards, Aani, Jaywan, Sanadak).
- Source URLs are cited inside each document.
- Regulatory content is UX translation, **not legal advice**. Verify against the CBUAE Rulebook before committing compliance-critical copy or flows.

## What Phase 02 builds on this

Token architecture, financial semantic model, component/pattern/journey taxonomies (detailed), accessibility architecture, localization architecture, multi-brand architecture — all constrained by the decisions recorded in this phase.
