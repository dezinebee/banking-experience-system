# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want BES to be a place where a first-time contributor filing an Arabic terminology correction is as welcome as a maintainer landing a component. We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that builds this project:

- Assuming good faith — CONTRIBUTING.md asks this of everyone, and it applies to how we read each other as much as how we write
- Giving critique on the work, with a reason: which principle, which spec section, which audit
- Accepting critique the same way, and changing your mind in public when you are wrong
- Being specific about uncertainty rather than confident about a guess — particularly on regulatory, accessibility, and Arabic-language questions
- Deferring to native speakers on Arabic copy and to people with lived experience on accessibility, rather than arguing from theory

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory jokes and language, public or private
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Personal or political attacks, and insulting or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or dismissing accessibility and localisation concerns as edge cases
- Any other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and code comments — and when an individual is officially representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers: repository → **Security** tab → **Report a vulnerability** works as a private channel, or contact the design authority named in GOVERNANCE.md directly.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainers will respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other members of the project's leadership.

### Enforcement guidelines

| Impact | Consequence |
|---|---|
| **Correction** — inappropriate language or unprofessional behaviour | A private, written warning explaining the violation. A public apology may be requested. |
| **Warning** — a single incident or a series of actions | A warning with consequences for continued behaviour, including no interaction with those involved for a specified period. |
| **Temporary ban** — a serious violation or sustained inappropriate behaviour | A temporary ban from any interaction with the project. |
| **Permanent ban** — a pattern of violation, harassment, or aggression toward individuals or groups | A permanent ban from any public interaction within the project. |
