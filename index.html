Financial experiences, engineered as a system

A domain-specific design system for banking and fintech in the UAE. Trust by design, clarity by default — money display, transaction states, fee disclosure, consent, fraud response and bilingual Arabic/English RTL design, specified as reusable foundations, components and patterns.
