Why this fee?

When does the money arrive?
Transfers to India usually arrive within 1 hour. Cut-off for same-day processing is 22:00 UAE time.

Session pause interruption

The z.critical security surface — no entrance animation, cannot be swiped away, never blames the user. Triggered here by button; in product, by screen-share/active-call detection.
